I am a physicist who applies computational methods to biological problems.

I currently work on generative and predictive models for antimicrobial peptides in Prof. Saugata Hazra's lab at IIT Roorkee, including [a latent-diffusion model and potency regressor](https://github.com/siddhantp2807/amp-challenge-2027) built for the [NeurIPS AMP Challenge](https://szczurek-lab.github.io/amp-challenge-website/). I am especially interested in interpretable models that connect protein sequence to properties like function, potency and toxicity. A secondary interest is small-molecule design using AI/ML for therapeutic use cases.

I did an integrated BS-MS in Physics at [IISER Mohali](https://www.iisermohali.ac.in/), India, followed by two years working in Dr. Rintu Kutum's Augumented Health Systems Lab at [Koita Centre for Digital Health at Ashoka (KCDH-A)](https://kcdha.ashoka.edu.in/). There I worked on EHR harmonization into OMOP CDM and LLM-based clinical information extraction, which gave me experience with messy real-world data.

I am seeking PhD opportunities for 2027 entry. My [CV](/assets/pdf/cv.pdf) has more detail.

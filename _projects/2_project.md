**Status: in progress, write-up pending.** Building a discovery pipeline for boronate pan-class-A β-lactamase inhibitors, combining:

- ECFP4 / Tanimoto novelty scoring against known chemical space
- Multi-criteria candidate ranking via the Derringer-Suich desirability function and `pymcdm` CRITIC weighting
- Candidate extraction from ChEMBL
- Ensemble structure building for BLSA (β-lactamase structural analysis)
